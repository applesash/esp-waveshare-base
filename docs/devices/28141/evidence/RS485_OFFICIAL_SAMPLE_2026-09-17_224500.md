# 28141 Official RS485 Validation: 2026-09-17

- Board identity: SKU 28141, MAC `14:c1:9f:4a:fd:a8`, port `/dev/ttyACM0`
- Adapter port: `/dev/ttyUSB0`
- Source: unmodified official 4.3B-BOX ESP-IDF `02_RS485_Test`
- Board UART: UART2, TX GPIO44, RX GPIO43
- Adapter framing: 115200 baud, 8N1, raw
- Console: USB Serial/JTAG; separate from RS485 traffic

## Result

The adapter transmitted `RS485-28141\r\n` and received the exact 13-byte echo:

```text
52 53 34 38 35 2d 32 38 31 34 31 0d 0a
```

RS485 byte-for-byte echo passed. The official sample uses no RTS, CTS, or manual DE/RE signal.
