- Card: user-reported 64 GB microSD
- Card inserted before boot
- Probe mode: native 1-bit SDMMC, GPIO2 CLK, GPIO1 CMD, GPIO4 D0
- Probe clock: ESP-IDF `SDMMC_FREQ_PROBING` (400 kHz)
- Probe operation: read-only card initialization; no format and no write

## Result


The card does not complete SD card electrical/protocol initialization even at the low probing clock. Therefore filesystem type, including exFAT, cannot yet be inspected by firmware. The failure occurs before FAT/exFAT mount handling.
